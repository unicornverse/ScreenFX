Appearance

An effect holds on screen for the chosen time, then clears itself. The dismiss button counts it down, and Esc ends one early. A keyboard shortcut plays a random effect without opening the popup.

Allow scheduled effects Off

Clicking the toolbar icon lets ScreenFX touch that one tab, for that one click. A schedule fires when you are not clicking anything, so it needs standing access to your tabs instead. ScreenFX never reads page content, and nothing leaves your browser. Turn this off any time.
